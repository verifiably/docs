Two passages in overview.md. (1) The worked example 'Does disease stage raise PHF19 expression in multiple myeloma?': 'disease stage raise' is unusual wording. Candidates: 'Does disease stage progression increase PHF19 gene expression levels?', or 'Is disease stage progression associated with higher PHF19 gene expression in patients, on average?'. (2) 'Subjective categories and labels': gene, disease and cell line names, ontology ones included, are names people or algorithms chose. Keep ontologies as trusted identifiers but state their limits rather than calling them not subjective.
